▸ Gate 1 — Critical Occlusion
M1 One-side visibility failure
CRITICAL_OCCLUSION
reliability(lm) =
count(vis ≥ 0.70 AND
 pres ≥ 0.70) ÷ total_frames

if reliability(lm) < 0.60 → reject
✕ FAIL
Knee · Hip · Ankle only
Compute median_visibility per landmark.

Left ≥1 joint ≤ 0.60 AND right side ≥ 0.60 → left message

Right ≥1 joint ≤ 0.60 AND left side ≥ 0.60 → right message
"Part of your [left/right] side was hidden from view"
Your [left/right] [knee/hip/ankle] wasn't clearly visible throughout the video. Rather than switching sides, rotate your camera slightly toward the front of your body — this brings your [left/right] side into view without losing the other.
 Re-film — see tips
M2 Both-sides visibility failure
CRITICAL_OCCLUSION
Same as M1
✕ FAIL
Knee · Hip · Ankle only
Both sides ≥1 joint with median_visibility ≤ 0.60 on left AND right
"We couldn't see your lower body clearly"
Your camera may be facing you from directly to your side or slightly behind you. Try angling it slightly toward the front of your body so both legs are fully in view throughout the squat.
 Re-film — see tips
M3 One-side presence failure
CRITICAL_OCCLUSION
Same as M1
✕ FAIL
Knee · Hip · Ankle only
median_visibility passes (> 0.60) but median_presence ≤ 0.50 for ≥1 joint on one side.

Left presence fails → left message
Right presence fails → right message
"Your [left/right] side kept moving out of frame"
Your [left/right] [knee/hip/ankle] wasn't fully in the frame throughout the video. Move the camera back slightly so your full body — [left/right] side included — stays visible throughout the squat.
 Re-film — see tips
▸ Gate 2 — Poor Composite Score
M4 Overall poor quality
POOR COMPOSITE
video_score = Σ weight(lm) × reliability(lm)

if video_score < 0.70 → reject
(no individual landmark hit 0.60 hard floor)
✕ FAIL
No additional logic needed. Composite score alone determines this message.

Typical causes: filming from behind, obstruction, low lighting.
"We couldn't read your body position clearly"
This usually happens when filming from behind, with something blocking the view, or in low lighting. Film from your side with good lighting and a clear background.
 Re-film — see tips

Make sure you're doing goblet squats and that your full body is in frame from the start of your first rep.

"Film a full set to get your analysis"
We need at least 3 complete reps to give you meaningful feedback. Squat all the way down and all the way back up for each one — don't stop at 1 or 2.
