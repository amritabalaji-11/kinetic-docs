⚡ TL;DR

"Kinetic is the first AI form coach that tracks you across weights — not just one rep, but your entire journey. It spots where your form breaks down as load increases, then tells you exactly how to fix it, because it knows your history."
Problem

Intermediate gym-goers training alone in small gyms have no reliable feedback loop on their form. They get injured, plateau, or develop bad habits for weeks — because there's nobody watching.

Solution

Upload a workout video with your weight. Kinetic uses computer vision and AI grounded in exercise science to score your form, identify joint-angle breakdowns, and tell you whether you're ready to progress — across your entire training history.

🔍 Context & Why Now

The Four Pain Points

01
Fear-driven plateau
A pain signal (e.g. knee pain on Goblet Squat) causes them to stay at the same weight for months, unsure if it's safe to progress.
02
Blind form drift
Bad form goes undetected for weeks or months until a stranger at the gym happens to mention it.
03
Lagging soreness signal
Unusual next-day soreness signals something was wrong — but the moment is already gone.
04
Invisible stagnation
Feeling "too comfortable" with no way to know if they're actually progressing or just going through the motions.

Current Workarounds

Users currently watch YouTube tutorials (generic, not personalised) or self-record and watch back (raw footage, no interpretation). Apps like Gymscore, Zing, and FormAI provide snapshot form feedback — they detect symptoms in a single video. None of them track form across weights over time or integrate workout logging. Kinetic is the first to connect form quality to progressive overload history.

💡 Solution Overview

Kinetic is a web app where users log workouts and upload exercise videos. The backend runs MediaPipe to extract joint angles, queries a RAG pipeline grounded in exercise science, and generates structured coaching. Every analysis is contextualised against the user's full history — Kinetic knows what your Goblet Squat looked like at 10kg, 15kg, and 20kg.

User Flow

1

Log in → Select exercise + enter weight

User selects exercise (e.g. Goblet Squat) and enters the weight used for this session.

2

Upload workout video

Upload video recorded from the session; guided by in-app filming tips for optimal analysis quality.

3

Kinetic processes video

MediaPipe extracts joint keypoints · RAG retrieves biomechanics context · Nemotron 3 Nano Omni generates structured JSON · Claude Sonnet produces coaching output

4

User sees results

Form score · Joint-angle corrections · Weight recommendation ("Your form at 12kg is solid. You're ready for 14kg.") · Comparison to past sessions

5

Session saved to history

All data persisted to the longitudinal data layer for future progression comparisons.

6

Dashboard shows form score trends

Track form scores across weights and time; habit loop drives weekly return.

🧩 Features & Scope

● Must-Have (P0) — MVP
1 · Video Upload & Processing Pipeline (Goblet Squat)
User uploads a video; backend runs MediaPipe pose detection and extracts rep-level biomechanical parameters (joint angles, stability, posture, rep count, rep time).
Acceptance Criteria

Video processed end-to-end in <30 seconds · Keypoints extracted accurately across rep range · Works on consumer-filmed video with provided filming guidelines

2 · AI Form Analysis Output
RAG-enhanced Nemotron 3 Nano Omni + Claude Sonnet generates a structured report: form score, joint-angle corrections, Movement Quality / Stability / Posture ratings, rep count, rep time, and specific coaching tips.
Acceptance Criteria

Output includes overall score · Minimum 2 specific joint corrections · At least 1 actionable coaching tip · ≥80% expert agreement in validation testing (Week 9)

3 · Weight-Based Progression Recommendation
Kinetic compares current session form against past sessions at lower weights and outputs a clear recommendation: "Ready to progress" / "Hold at current weight" / "Drop weight and fix X first".
Acceptance Criteria

Recommendation generated when ≥2 sessions logged for the same exercise · Logic validated against expert assessment

4 · Workout Logging (Builder & Tracker)
Users log workouts — exercise, sets, reps, weight — linked to form analysis sessions. Creates the longitudinal data layer that powers progression recommendations.
Acceptance Criteria

User can create, save, and retrieve workout logs · Logs linked to form sessions in history

5 · Session History & Dashboard
Chronological view of all form sessions with score trends, weight progression, and side-by-side comparisons across sessions. Builds the habit loop.
Acceptance Criteria

Dashboard displays form score trend by exercise across weights and time · Filterable by exercise

6 · User Authentication & Profile
Sign-up/login, basic profile (exercise preferences, training frequency). Required to persist and personalise longitudinal data.
Acceptance Criteria

User can sign up, log in, and all sessions persist to their account

Out of Scope

Multiple exercises beyond Goblet Squat
Accuracy must be validated deeply on 1 exercise first. Shoulder Press is a stretched goal only if Goblet Squat hits ≥80% by Week 9.
Real-time live camera analysis
Pipeline latency (MediaPipe + Nemotron + Claude Sonnet) makes real-time infeasible. "Record" in-browser is async, not live.
Mobile native app
Web-first (responsive) for POC. Mobile post-validation.
Nutrition / general fitness tracking
Outside core value prop. Competitive and crowded space.
B2B creator portal
Phase 2 initiative. Requires validated B2C user base first.

⚙️ Technical Approach

Frontend
React + Tailwind
Responsive web app. Web-first to validate the full pipeline end-to-end as a POC before native mobile investment.
Pose Detection
MediaPipe (Python)
Extracts 33 joint keypoints per rep. Computes joint angles (knee, hip, torso), stability, posture, rep count, rep time.
Stage 1 AI — Video Analysis
Nemotron 3 Nano Omni
Receives raw video AND MediaPipe biomechanics JSON as dual inputs. Cross-references visual context against computed joint data for grounded analysis.
Stage 2 AI — Coaching
Claude Sonnet 4.6
Receives Nemotron output + RAG context + user weight history. Produces coaching output and weight progression recommendation.
Knowledge Layer
RAG — Vector DB
Biomechanics corpus indexed for retrieval: research papers, YouTube transcripts, exercise tutorials, muscle anatomy images. TBD: Pinecone / Weaviate / pgvector.
Infra & Storage
GCP + S3 / Cloud Storage
Python API on GCP. API Gateway. GCS/S3 for video files. Structured DB for exercise logs, form scores, angles, rep data, user history.

🧪 Iterative Pipeline Testing Plan

Rather than testing the AI pipeline only at the end (Week 9), Kinetic runs progressive tests each week — adding one input at a time to isolate where the pipeline breaks down early. Results are logged in GitHub /tests/weekly-results/ and summarised in a shared doc each Friday.

📝 User Stories

US1 As an intermediate gym-goer, I want to upload my Goblet Squat video with my weight, so I can get specific feedback on what I'm doing wrong and whether it's safe to increase weight.
US2 As a user stuck at the same weight due to knee pain, I want to know exactly which joint angle is causing the issue, so I can fix it confidently and progress.
US3 As a regular user, I want to see how my form score has changed across weights over time, so I can see that I'm actually improving — not just going through the motions.
US4 As a user who trains alone, I want a single app that logs my workouts and analyses my form, so I don't have to switch between tools during my session.

❓ Open Questions

Owner: Research Lead — Biomechanics research must define ideal angle ranges for knee, hip, and torso before accuracy can be validated
What is the minimum number of sessions before a progression recommendation is surfaced?
Owner: Engineering + Product — Too few sessions = unreliable; too many = frustrating delay
Who are the human experts validating form scores?
Owner: Founder — Certified PT, physiotherapist, or sports scientist? Must be confirmed before Week 9
How are user videos stored and for how long?
Owner: Engineering + Legal — Videos contain biometric data; retention policy and privacy compliance needed before launch
What confidence threshold triggers a "low certainty" flag vs. full analysis output?
Owner: Engineering — Needs calibration from validation testing
Freemium limit: 2 or 3 uploads per week during trial?
Owner: Product — Affects activation rate vs. conversion incentive trade-off
